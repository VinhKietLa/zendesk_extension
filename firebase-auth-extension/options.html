Help & Support

Getting Started

Agent Hero helps you manage Zendesk tickets more efficiently with reminders, macros, and productivity tools.

Key Features

  • Important Tickets: Add tickets to your important list with reminders
  • Pinned Tickets: Keep frequently accessed tickets at the top
  • Quick Macros: Save and use common responses
  • Auto-Refresh: Keep tickets updated automatically
  • Notifications: Get alerts for important tickets

Pro Features

  • Unlimited pinned tickets and macros
  • Email reminders
  • Working hours mode
  • Cloud sync across devices
  • Priority support
